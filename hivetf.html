FAQ — HiveFlow V1

What does HiveFlow do?

Fast, lightweight explorer for HIVE transfers only (not HBD). Scans account history via public Hive RPCs and lets you filter by direction and amount.

How to filter IN < 0.5 HIVE?

Search a username, then set: Show = IN only, that are = less than, value = 0.5. List auto-filters. Great for finding dust tips or bot tests.

Why only HIVE?

Intentionally filters out HBD and other ops to keep focus on HIVE flow analysis.

How does copy work?

Click the @username pill in each card to copy. Click memo to expand.

Is private data exposed?

No. Uses public condenser_api.get_account_history. No login, no keys.

Why does scroll stop?

V1 loads ~100 HIVE transfers first for speed. Keep scrolling to scan deeper history.
